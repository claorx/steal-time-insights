Why the dashboard and saved profiles differ +

Different outcome: the one-time TutorialOrder funnels continue across sessions. A player can complete later and count against their original funnel-entry date. The profile comparison below counts only their first saved session in the current save generation.

Different coverage: Roblox shows Oct 23–29; profile history starts with the Oct 27 save generation. Resetting saves does not establish a fresh Roblox analytics history. “Fresh profile” does not necessarily mean a first-ever player.

Different clocks: the dashboard reported its latest update at Oct 29, 10:00 AM (as displayed). Production profiles were read from 11:06–11:12 AM Toronto time. Active progress may not yet have saved, and Roblox processes events separately.

Different records: the game saves progress before its analytics queue delivers the event. The adapter can discard failed or queued events. Historical delivery losses cannot be quantified from these saves, so this audit does not claim exact reconciliation.

How the comparison works

Only eligible, randomized profiles with no prior saved playtime in this save generation and assigned to tutorial_order_v2 are in the before/after cohorts. They are not necessarily first-ever visitors. A player belongs to the cohort of their original enrollment, even if they later return after the update.

First-session rates exclude unfinished or missing first-session records. Completion uses the saved ST_FirstSessionCompleted flag; intermediate steps use their saved cumulative playtime against the recorded first-session duration.

Intervals are 95% Wilson intervals; percentage-point differences use Newcombe intervals. These describe sampling uncertainty, not release causality.

Limits that matter

  • The cutoff is approximate. Profiles do not identify the running release version; old servers may overlap the publish. Try the ±10-minute exclusion.
  • A before/after comparison is observational. Traffic mix, time of day, other changes, and server rollout can affect the results.
  • The current save generation began October 27. Earlier profile history is outside this extraction; Roblox event history may span that reset.
  • These profiles do not retain exit reasons, detailed startup timing, or UI impressions. The official entry funnel adds event reach counts, but cannot identify causes of abandonment.
  • Post-update day-one retention has not matured. Missing or immature values are not zero.
