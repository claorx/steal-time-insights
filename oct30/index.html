RECOMMENDATIONS · GAME UNCHANGED

A SCENARIO, NOT A FORECAST

What if fewer hits ended without a recorded kill?

Assume the same entrants and the observed downstream completion ratio stays fixed.

Some of the 54 may still be in progress or missing telemetry. Recovered players may behave differently. The slider is arithmetic under stated assumptions, not an estimate of the treatment’s effect.

HOW TO KNOW A FIX WORKED

One focused change.
One stable experiment.

  1. Freeze the schema and assignment. Add a release/version marker to future analysis; retain tutorial type, order and UTC enrollment tags. Audit existing stage-exit, retry and ObservationGap signals before adding duplicate events.
  2. Use a common outcome. Compare 5-minute first-session survival and unaided post-tutorial actions. Tutorial completion alone is not comparable across the old and new lesson requirements.
  3. Let the cohorts mature. Compare the same entry hours, platform and acquisition source; wait for first-session ends and the next-day return window. Report missing data separately.
  4. Choose a stopping rule in advance. As a planning example, detecting 20% → 25% first-session survival needs roughly 1,100 independent sessions per arm at 80% power / 5% two-sided significance. Recalculate for the actual baseline and avoid repeatedly declaring a winner.

The counting rules that matter

  • Funnel reach is not proof of causation or a count of users who permanently quit.
  • One-time tutorials can advance on later visits. Session milestone funnels viewed By user can qualify from return sessions.
  • V3 waits for a contiguous observed prefix. Missing UI acknowledgements can hold back later displayed steps.
  • Legacy detail funnels can credit skipped earlier checkpoints when a later authoritative milestone arrives. Matched attempt counts do not establish 100% objective success.
  • The analytics adapter queues events and can drop or fail deliveries. A historical loss cannot be quantified from this capture.
  • The new:2026-10-30 enrollment tag starts at 8 p.m. Toronto on Oct 29 and spans the reported 1 a.m. release. No device-adjusted comparison, session-level duration variance, or mature post-update D1 outcome was obtained. No mobile-only or statistical winner claim is made.
